Soplo: learn the recorder

Soplo is a recorder method for iPhone and iPad. You play your real recorder and the app listens through the microphone: the score waits until you get each note and, when it doesn't sound, tells you why (blowing too hard, too softly, a hole not fully covered, or a different note). 30 lessons, from breathing and the first B to the high notes and the alto recorder, with 26 traditional school songs from many countries, a tuner and a fingering chart for Baroque and German recorders.

The first two chapters (breathing, tonguing, B, A and G, with their songs) are free. The full course is unlocked with a single payment: no subscription, no ads, forever, and shared with Family Sharing.

Questions

Which recorder do I need?

A soprano (descant) recorder, the school one. The last chapter of the course is for the alto. The app asks with a picture whether your recorder has Baroque or German fingering, and you can change it in Settings.

My recorder sounds a little sharp or flat

That's normal: many school recorders are not at 440 Hz. In “Your recorder” you play an A and Soplo learns how far yours is off and applies it everywhere.

Does the app record me?

No. The microphone sound is analysed on your device as you play and is never stored or sent anywhere.

I changed devices and can't see my purchase

Open a locked chapter and tap “Restore purchase”, or do it in Settings, with the same Apple Account. Your progress travels through iCloud.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything is inside the app. You only need a connection to buy or restore.

Privacy: Soplo collects no data at all. No accounts, no ads, no analytics. The microphone is analysed on your device and never recorded. Privacy policy
