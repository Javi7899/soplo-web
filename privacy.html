Privacy policy

Soplo · Last updated: 16 November 2026

Soplo

What data Soplo collects

None. Soplo has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it sends nothing to any server of the developer.

The microphone

Soplo uses the microphone to hear your recorder and tell you whether each note sounds right. The sound is analysed on your device as you play: it is not recorded, not stored and never leaves the device. You can withdraw the permission in iOS Settings; the course can be followed without listening.

Your progress

Finished lessons, stars and your recorder's settings are stored on your device and, if iCloud is on, in the iCloud key-value store of your Apple Account, so they follow you on iPhone and iPad. Apple manages that copy and only you can access it; the developer cannot read it.

Purchases

The course purchase is processed by Apple. The developer does not receive your payment details or your identity.

Children

Soplo is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.

Contact

For any question about this policy, write to the address on the support page.
